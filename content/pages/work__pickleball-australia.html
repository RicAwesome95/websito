Pickleball Australia

The pulse that powers pickleball.

What began as a backyard game with two paddles, a ball, and some tape has grown into one of Australia’s fastest-rising sports, bringing together players of all ages and abilities. As the national governing body, Pickleball Australia needed a brand that could unite a fast-moving community while celebrating the sport’s distinct personality: joyful, rebellious, inclusive, and proudly different.

Framing

Strategic framingStakeholder engagement

We framed the challenge around capturing the unique duality at the heart of pickleball – balancing its grassroots, backyard origins with its rapid rise toward national championships and international stage representation. The goal was to establish a brand platform capable of guiding an expanding ecosystem of players, affiliated clubs, and sanctioned tournaments while maintaining the sport's accessible, open spirit.

Immersing

Organisational immersionCustomer & audience insightCultural & contextual insight

We engaged closely with key stakeholders and the broader pickleball community to understand what drives its explosive growth. By exploring the culture behind casual driveway rallies and competitive leagues, we uncovered a passionate community united by play rather than pedigree. This insight defined the organisation's true strategic role – acting not just as a traditional administrator, but as the energetic heartbeat backing the people who bring the game to life.

Solving

Brand strategyBrand architecture

We defined a core strategic positioning around the central idea: "The pulse that powers pickleball." We established a unified brand architecture that respects the makeshift spirit of the game while providing the structure required of a national governing body. The strategy positions Pickleball Australia as a spirited movement leader that connects, energises, and welcomes players across every level of participation.

Actioning

Brand IdentityBrand guidelinesDesign systems & implementation

We translated the strategic direction into a dynamic, flexible identity system. Built from the fundamentals of play – line, shape, rhythm, and connection – the brandmark combines bold geometry with a dynamic footprint to convey both grassroots energy and national stature. Motion lines, expressive typography, vibrant colours, and comprehensive brand guidelines were engineered to ensure seamless application across digital channels, merchandise, and event touchpoints.

Partnering with Pickleball Australia, we delivered a comprehensive rebrand to support the sport's remarkable national growth. Our engagement spanned stakeholder engagement, brand strategy, brand architecture, visual identity development, brand guidelines, and multi-touchpoint brand application.

We created a cohesive visual and verbal framework tailored to connect a diverse stakeholder network – ranging from local community venues and grassroots players to commercial partners and international sports bodies. The resulting brand system provides the tools necessary to maintain consistency and high visibility across nationwide marketing campaigns, digital platforms, and tournament environments.

To build a brand capable of leading a rapidly expanding movement, we anchored the design thinking in the rhythm and energy of the game itself. The brandmark serves as an abstract tribute to the sport's origins – drawings from driveway tape, borrowed paddles, and simple geometry – while maintaining the structural balance and weight expected of a national governing body.

The design system channels this raw energy through expressive typography, bold color palettes, and motion graphics that reflect the warmth, diversity, and pace of the pickleball community. By positioning Pickleball Australia as "the pulse that powers pickleball," the brand balances authority with accessibility, giving the organisation a confident identity to carry the sport into its next chapter on the global stage.
