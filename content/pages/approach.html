01.

Framing

Framing the objectives you are trying to achieve.

We start by understanding what the organisation is trying to achieve, what is getting in the way and where brand and marketing can create the greatest value. That means looking beyond the immediate brief, to the business, the market, the customer, the evidence and the strategies already in play. We bring those perspectives together, test the assumptions behind the challenge and establish a clear definition of success. The result is a sharper problem to solve, greater alignment around what matters and a clear foundation for everything that follows.


Services

We bring the evidence together to define the problem worth solving. This means challenging assumptions, identifying the most important opportunities and creating alignment around a clear strategic question that can guide the work that follows.

We establish a clear link between the work and the value it is expected to create. By defining organisational objectives, the drivers of success and meaningful measures upfront, we create a framework for evaluating impact and return.

02.

IMMERSING

Immersing ourselves in your world.

We get close to the organisation, the category and the people who matter. That means understanding what is already known, hearing different perspectives and looking beyond the obvious to uncover the forces shaping behaviour, choice and performance. We combine internal knowledge with customer, market and cultural insight to build a richer picture of the opportunity. By connecting evidence from across the organisation and outside it, we identify the tensions, patterns and possibilities that will give the strategy something meaningful to respond to.

Services

We spend time inside the organisation to understand its ambitions, culture, capabilities and constraints. Through leadership and stakeholder engagement, workshops and reviewing existing materials, we build a grounded view of the business as it really operates.

We explore what people think, feel and do, and, importantly, why. Using qualitative and quantitative research, behavioural insight and existing customer data, we uncover the needs, motivations and barriers that shape decision-making.

We look closely at the market around you: how competitors are positioned, where conventions have formed and where sameness is creating opportunity. The aim is not simply to understand who else is there, but to identify where meaningful advantage can be built.

Brands and organisations do not operate in isolation. We examine the wider cultural, social, political and behavioural shifts shaping expectations, attitudes and choices, helping us understand not just where the market is now, but where it may be moving next.

03.

SOLVING

Solving problems with a tailored blend of capabilities.

We turn insight into a clear strategic response, bringing together the right combination of thinking around the outcome we are trying to achieve. Rather than treating brand, marketing, customer and creative strategy as separate disciplines, we look at how they need to work together to create the greatest impact. The result is strategy that creates focus: clear choices about what to do, when to do it, and what to prioritise to build advantage. Every recommendation is grounded in the evidence, connected to the organisation’s objectives and designed to give teams a practical path forward.

Services

We define the role the brand needs to play in achieving the organisation’s ambitions; from positioning and value proposition to brand architecture, pillars and the ideas that create meaningful differentiation.

We define how brands, sub-brands, products and services should relate to one another, creating a clear structure that makes the portfolio easier to manage, easier to understand and better able to support growth.

We determine how marketing can best contribute to organisational objectives, making clear choices around audiences, priorities, investment and the role different activities need to play.

We define what needs to be communicated, to whom, and in what order; creating a clear framework for messages, audiences and channels so communications activity supports the wider organisational and brand strategy.

We translate broader strategy into a focused plan for action, defining the campaign objective, audience, proposition, messaging and role of channels to ensure creative activity is built around a clear outcome.

We use customer insight and behavioural science to understand what will influence choice and action, then translate that understanding into strategies that build preference, remove friction and change behaviour.

We turn strategic direction into a clear creative platform: defining the ideas, messages and principles that will make the strategy distinctive, compelling and useful across channels and experiences.

We define what makes an organisation distinctive and valuable as a place to work, connecting employee needs and expectations with the organisation’s purpose, culture and ambitions.

We identify where new value can be created through products, services, experiences or business models, then turn those opportunities into a clear roadmap for development.

04.

ACTIONING

Actioning the tools to both ensure and measure success.

We turn strategic direction into ideas, identities, experiences and communications that actually achieve the organisation's and project’s objectives. Every creative decision is grounded in the strategy that came before it, so the work is not simply distinctive, it has a clear job to do. We create the tools and expressions that put strategy into action. The aim is consistency without sameness: work that can flex across audiences, channels and moments while continuing to build towards the same outcomes.

Services

We translate positioning into a distinctive visual and verbal identity, creating the assets, systems and principles that make the strategy recognisable and usable across every touchpoint.

We develop creative platforms and campaigns that turn strategy into compelling ideas, giving audiences a reason to notice, understand and act.

We design digital experiences that bring the brand to life in practical, intuitive and distinctive ways, ensuring the strategy holds together wherever people encounter it.

We build flexible systems, guidelines and tools that help organisations apply the brand consistently and confidently, while giving internal teams and partners what they need to keep it working over time.

We translate strategy into clear, engaging communications across channels and audiences, ensuring what the organisation says is consistent with what it stands for and what it is trying to achieve.
