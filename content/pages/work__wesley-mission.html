A brand on a mission.

With over 200 years of pioneering social service and championing the voices of those often overlooked, Wesley Mission needed a brand refresh to reflect the depth and boldness of their work. We reimagined their outdated identity into a dynamic new emblem that signals momentum and reflects their driving commitment to ‘do more good’.

Framing

Strategic framing

We established a strategic framework grounded in Wesley Mission’s ‘Case for Change’ and 2028 Strategic Plan. We defined the brief around solving core market challenges: overcoming low brand understanding despite a 200+ year heritage, updating an outdated pre-digital identity system, and elevating brand presence to drive growth in an increasingly competitive social services sector.

Immersing

Organisational immersionCustomer & audience insightCategory & competitor analysis

Through extensive stakeholder engagement and service architecture review, we immersed ourselves in the breadth of Wesley Mission's community services – from Lifeline and housing advocacy to addiction, disability, and elder care. This deep organisational immersion revealed a relentless driving spirit grounded in faith, hope, and compassion, highlighting the need to connect their historic legacy with modern, proactive advocacy.

Solving

Brand strategyBrand architectureCommunications strategy

We developed a single-minded internal brand platform anchored in the core positioning: “Driven to do more good.” By streamlining their brand platform and messaging matrix, we bridged the gap between Wesley Mission’s faith-based roots and its frontline social action – positioning the organisation as a courageous, compassionate advocate confronting inequity across Australia.

Actioning

We translated the strategy into a bold new emblem – reimagining the historic Wesley Dove using dynamic geometry. Embedded negative-space arrow and subtle heart motifs merge strength with compassion, creating a distinctive, digital-first design system. Supported by brand guidelines, voice frameworks, website consultation, and application tools, the new identity ensures cut-through across digital and physical touchpoints.

The team brought strong strategic creative capability, deep expertise, professionalism and genuine commitment, and made collaboration between our office locations (Melbourne and Sydney) seamless.
