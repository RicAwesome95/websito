Diversity Council Australia

Forging a courageous new identity for diversity and inclusion.

Diversity Council Australia, a leading voice for workplace diversity and inclusion, needed a refreshed brand strategy and visual identity to reflect its growing role in shaping stronger organisations and a stronger society. Positioned at the intersection of courage and pragmatism, the brand reflects DCA’s drive to break barriers and lead important, often challenging conversations with empathy and expertise.

Framing

Strategic framing

We established a strategic foundation centred on transforming DCA from an awareness-led reporter into a provider of industry-leading business intelligence. We framed the core challenge around bridging human-centred advocacy with pragmatic B2B leadership – setting performance-based objectives to scale member reach, diversify revenue streams, and amplify research influence across the Australian workforce.

Immersing

Organisational immersionCultural & contextual insightCustomer & audience insight

We conducted extensive stakeholder engagement and lived experience research to ground the creative process in authentic workplace realities. By exploring the perspectives of employees, organisational leaders, and decision-makers, we identified a vital need for DCA to lead more impactful, uncomfortable national conversations while providing accessible, evidence-based tools for its member network.

Solving

Brand strategyBrand architectureCommunications strategy

We developed a brand narrative centred on the positioning: "The Courageous Voice for a Stronger Society." This strategic response merges research rigour with conviction – positioning DCA as an empathetic peak body that breaks down complex evidence into actionable workplace steps, empowering Australian organisations to move beyond the status quo.

Actioning

Brand IdentityDesign systems & implementation

We translated the strategy into a bold, symbolic visual expression that moves away from literal corporate tropes. Featuring custom, outward-pushing letterforms that symbolise momentum, determination, and breakthrough, the revitalised identity system – supported by brand guidelines, digital consultation, and launch video assets—gives DCA the authority and gravitas to lead nationwide change.

Partnering with Diversity Council Australia, we undertook an end-to-end brand transformation. Our scope encompassed stakeholder engagement, service design, creative consultation, brand strategy, brand architecture, tone of voice development, visual identity design, brand guidelines, brand video production, website consultation, and multi-channel applications.

To support DCA's evolution as Australia's peak inclusion body, we developed a comprehensive suite of design tools and communication frameworks. This system was engineered to deliver consistent B2B authority across business intelligence reports, member resources, digital touchpoints, and public advocacy campaigns.

To position DCA at the intersection of courage and pragmatism, we moved the brand from a literal design expression to a symbolic, purpose-led system. The primary brandmark features custom letterforms that actively push outward – a visual representation of breaking through complacency, embracing complex conversations, and driving progress.

By balancing creative distinction with peak-body gravitas, the identity reflects DCA's role in merging evidence with conviction. The visual system and brand voice equip DCA to lead challenging conversations with empathy, providing a clear visual language that connects with corporate leaders, decision-makers, and the broader community alike.
