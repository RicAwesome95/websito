Nyaal Banyul

Gather round to experience a new perspective.

Nyaal Banyul Geelong Convention and Event Centre pays homage to the site’s history on the iconic Western Beach cliffs in Djilang (Geelong). For thousands of years, the Wadawurrung People have come together here to yarn, celebrate, share songs and dance, trade, make decisions, solve problems and pass on knowledge.

Framing

Strategic framingStakeholder engagement

The project required elevating a major regional infrastructure precinct with empathy, deep cultural respect, and absolute nuance. We framed the strategic brief around balancing two critical imperatives: honouring the Wadawurrung cultural heritage of Djilang while establishing a clear foundation for a modern destination capable of driving economic and civic value for the Bellarine region.

Immersing

Research & insightOrganisational immersionCultural & contextual insight

We immersed ourselves in the Wadawurrung narrative of 'Layers of Country' – encompassing Below Country, Above Country, Water Country, Mountain Country, and Sky Country – through continuous, respectful engagement with the Wadawurrung People and venue architects. This deep contextual grounding ensured every strategic choice respected the gifted name, Nyaal Banyul, and reflected the site's rich history as a place of gathering.

Solving

Brand strategyBrand voiceMessaging matrix

We resolved the strategic challenge by positioning Nyaal Banyul around the central idea: 'Gather Round to Experience a New Perspective'. Rather than treating cultural heritage as a static motif, we defined a strategy where the brand acts as an empathetic host and open vessel – celebrating Country while seamlessly accommodating a diverse spectrum of commercial, community, and civic events.

Actioning

Brand IdentityDesign systems & implementation

We translated the strategy into an iconic visual identity and flexible design system that moves beyond tokenistic symbology. The assets, tone of voice guidelines, and digital frameworks were engineered to flex effortlessly – allowing the brand to shift from a bold lead host to an understated backdrop, maintaining saliency and consistency across every touchpoint.

Partnering with the Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Trust (MCET) and the Wadawurrung Traditional Owners Aboriginal Corporation (WTOAC), we delivered an end-to-end branding program for the new Nyaal Banyul Geelong Convention and Event Centre. Our engagement encompassed extensive stakeholder orchestration, overarching brand strategy, voice and key messaging development, visual identity design, comprehensive brand guidelines, and digital consultation.

To ensure long-term operational success, we developed a robust suite of brand tools and modular design assets tailored for multi-channel deployment. The scope required navigating complex civic, cultural, and commercial stakeholder landscapes to align the precinct’s commercial ambitions with a genuine commitment to cultural respect and place-based storytelling.

To build a venue brand capable of representing both ancient heritage and modern ambition, we anchored the identity in the action of perspective – directly reflecting the gifted Wadawurrung name Nyaal Banyul ('open your eyes to the hills'). We deliberately avoided generic cultural iconography, choosing instead to create a visual system that honours the 'Layers of Country' narrative through nuanced design elements and sensitive storytelling.

The resulting identity functions as a true 'vessel brand'. Through a modular visual system and empathetic brand voice, Nyaal Banyul can seamlessly adapt to its environment – stepping forward with confident lead presence for major global conventions, or adopting a supportive, host role when accommodating community gatherings and artistic performances. The brand extends an open invitation to explore groundbreaking ideas, connect to Country, and leave with a renewed perspective.

The Contenders delivered highly successfully on both challenges, creating a brand that we are very proud of through a very collaborative and agile process, and engaging and understanding traditional owners authentically to pay homage to the cultural significance and history of the place the centre stands on.

Elizabeth Kozmevski — General Marketing Manager
